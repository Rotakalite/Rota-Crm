The Rota-CRM system is a client consumption data management platform with dashboards, analytics, and Clerk-based role-based authentication (admin/client). Core features include multi-level folders, a training calendar, branding, Gmail notifications, document counting, and bulk email notifications. Future modules are planned for Elite consumption analysis, Carbon Footprint Tracking (DEFRA), Guest Engagement, AI Analytics, Cultural Sustainability, and Waste Management. Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) via email is integrated, and a Tedarikçi Modülü (Supplier Module) is a priority.

The primary problem addressed in this trajectory was the unreliable document management. Users explicitly requested the ability to upload single or bulk PDF documents, save them permanently, and download them in their original format to specific folders, along with client selection. Previous issues included documents downloading as TXT files, undefined training names, Invalid Date displays, and 404/500 server errors during download or silent upload failures where the frontend reported success but the backend failed to persist the document. The user explicitly requested a complete re-design of the document management module from scratch to ensure reliability and fix these issues.
