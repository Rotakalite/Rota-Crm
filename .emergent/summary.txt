<product_requirements>
The Rota-CRM is a multi-role SaaS platform designed for client consumption data management, featuring interactive dashboards and Clerk-based authentication. The primary objective is to empower different user roles—Admin, Client, and Consultant—with appropriate access levels and functionalities. Key features include:
1.  **Email System Refinement**: Enhancing email notifications for document and training uploads, ensuring accurate data display, preventing bulk sending to irrelevant parties, and supporting both individual and bulk notifications with detailed content (document name, upload date, folder; training name, date, instructor, time).
2.  **Supplier Management**: Implementation of full CRUD operations for supplier information (company, address, category, local/non-local status, certifications, monthly purchases), complemented by a local/non-local ratio pie chart for analytical insights.
3.  **Personnel Management**: Providing full CRUD capabilities for personnel details (name, surname, position, certifications, local/non-local status, gender), along with local/non-local and male/female ratio pie charts for reporting.
4.  **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**: Client users are strictly limited to viewing their own data, while Admin users possess comprehensive CRUD access across the platform. Client selection is to be automated for CLIENT roles.
5.  **Authentication Persistence**: Addressing and resolving recurring logout issues and 401 unauthorized errors to ensure a stable user experience.
6.  **Sustainability Targets Module**: Enabling users to set, track, and report on measurable sustainability goals and progress.
7.  **Delete Functionality**: Implementing delete options for Personnel and Supplier records.
8.  **Consultant Management System**: Allowing Consultant users to register, manage, and interact with data of their assigned clients. Initially, client data was view-only for consultants, but this has been expanded to full CRUD capabilities. ROTA is a designated default consultant requiring a dedicated dashboard.
</product_requirements>
